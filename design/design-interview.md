## README accuracy pass

**Q:** What should the front-door bullet say about the operator amplitude envelope?
**A:** Name the family briefly. The bullet now reads "An amplitude LFO over a
warm-up, a surge and two decay stages, with four modes." The contract carries the
release and the mode names.

**Q:** Which of the thirteen waveforms came from which chip, and which one is your own shape?
**A:** Every OPZ and OPL3 waveform is in the set, plus the new `rect tri`. The
tables are generated from formulas, not lifted from the chips, so "modelled after"
describes the coverage of the set rather than a sample copy.

**Q:** Who has played or heard it, and what would count as confirmation?
**A:** Nobody else has played or heard it yet, and I haven't designed a test. The
results are my own listening so far, so the front door says that plainly instead of
calling it a pending confirmation.

**Q:** What does the current wordmark get wrong, and which reference points at the fix?
**A:** Zen Dots and Unbounded are OFL and fit the spirit, but the alpha is not a real
alpha and does not look like one, because I could not find an OFL family with the
Greek alphabet in the shape I wanted. Zen Dots caps look odd in a way that is not
futuristic or techno to me. I want something more custom, in the spirit of the Foley
Room-era Amon Tobin mark, or Rexlia, which is roughly contemporary. Eurostile, Stop,
Corporate and VDL's Line and Giga are typefaces I admire for the character, not
models to trace.

The TDR line was a Catalan slip. The README now says "Wipeout and Autechre covers".

**Q:** Does the custom mark keep the TYPE P. plus alpha construction, and which direction leads the first sketch?
**A:** I don't know yet; I need to experiment. A cut-up and layered Rexlia could
work. The alpha can be built from existing font components, since most free fonts
have no Greek glyphs. Vector art is probably better than an embedded font, and it
makes licensing easier.
